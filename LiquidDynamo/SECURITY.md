# Security Policy

## Reporting a Vulnerability

Agrigence and the community take security bugs in LiquidDynamo seriously. We appreciate your efforts to responsibly disclose your findings, and will make every effort to acknowledge your contributions.

To report a security issue, please contact security@agrigence.com or open a security advisory.

Agrigence will send a response indicating the next steps in handling your report. After the initial reply to your report, we will keep you informed of the progress towards a fix and full announcement, and may ask for additional information or guidance.

Report security bugs in third-party dependencies to the person or team maintaining the package or dependency.
